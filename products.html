Product & Trade Information

Every product is Halaal certified and baked at our HACCP food-safety audited Diep River factory, where our recipes are produced on modern bakery equipment to a consistent standard. All products carry a 12-month shelf life in recyclable packaging.

Allergens: all our biscuits contain Wheat, Gluten, Soya and Cow's milk. Full ingredients, nutrition and pack/case specifications are in our trade catalogue.
