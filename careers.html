Join Our Team

We're a close-knit team of bakers, packers, and food lovers who take real pride in what we make. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

Est. 2002  ·  Diep River, Cape Town

What We Stand For

Life at The Cookie Factory

We've been baking since 2002 and our culture reflects that — grounded, consistent, and proud of a job done well.

Craftsmanship

We take pride in every batch. Quality isn't a checkbox — it's how we've built our reputation since 2002.

Community

We're a tight team. Everyone knows their role and looks out for each other — from the bakery floor to the factory store.

Passion

We genuinely love what we do. If you care about quality food and honest work, you'll fit right in.

Integrity

We use quality ingredients and honest processes. We don't cut corners in the bakery — and we don't cut corners with our people either.

Growth

We invest in the people who invest in us. Many of our team members have grown their roles alongside the business.

Consistency

Our customers come back because they know exactly what to expect. We value reliability in our products — and our team.
