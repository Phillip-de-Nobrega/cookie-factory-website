Our Story

No two biscuits ever look exactly the same

Since 1998, The Cookie Factory has been crafting biscuits the way they were always meant to be made — by hand, with care, and with quality ingredients you can taste in every bite.

Operating from our factory in Diep River, Cape Town, our master bakers take pride in producing biscuits with a home-style character that sets us apart. Made with stone ground flour and real ingredients, each batch carries the warmth of traditional baking.

From our classic Ginger and Coconut cookies to our indulgent Chocolate Creams, every product is a testament to craft — unique, as home baking should be.

"We pride ourselves on never having two biscuits look exactly the same — each one carefully crafted by a master baker."
